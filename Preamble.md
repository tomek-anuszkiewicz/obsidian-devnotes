# Preamble — Scope, Empirical Grounding, and Evolution of This Vault

> **Current as of:** September 11, 2026

## Nature of This Knowledge Base

The notes, frameworks, and architectural models documented across this vault represent the **living state of the author's thoughts, mental models, and engineering philosophy** as of September 11, 2026.

This repository is neither an encyclopedic catalog of computer science trivia nor an academic treatise on hypothetical architectures. Instead, it is an **empirically grounded personal knowledge base**, strictly governed by several core operating principles:

---

## 1. Hands-On Exploration and Direct Experience

The topics addressed within these notes focus exclusively on areas where the author has **active, practical engagement**:
- Subsystems and architectures the author has directly built, refactored, benchmarked, or operated.
- Technologies explored through intensive, hands-on spike investigations and real-world integration harnesses.
- Domains where the author possesses direct professional experience, structural intuition, and meaningful mechanical familiarity.

---

## 2. Rejection of Detached Abstractions

A foundational commitment of this vault is **the avoidance of empty, detached theory**:
- We deliberately avoid speculative discussions about abstract architectures that have no empirical anchor in real hardware, compilers, operating systems, or running software.
- Concepts must survive contact with reality: memory and CPU cache realities, database execution plans, profilers, deterministic test harnesses, and actual operational failures.
- Every architectural claim is held accountable to real hardware, database, and system runtime behavior (as outlined in [[The 5-Layer System Stack for Agentic Software Engineering]]).

---

## 3. Dynamic and Evolving Scope

The breadth, depth, and inventory of topics captured here is **explicitly non-static**:
- The knowledge base will continuously expand, deepen, and shift as the author's hands-on experience broadens and new technical frontiers are explored.
- As new experiments yield empirical data, existing notes will be refactored, updated, or challenged to reflect deeper insights.
- The repository mirrors an active engineering journey: practical challenges encountered today seed the architectural theorems of tomorrow.
